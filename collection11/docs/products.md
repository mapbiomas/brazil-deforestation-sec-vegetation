# Products — Collection 11

**MapBiomas Brazil · Deforestation and Secondary Vegetation · 1985–2025 series**

The temporal rules are in [temporal-rules.md](temporal-rules.md). This document describes what
comes out of them: the chain of internal products, what was published, and how each pixel is
encoded.

---

## 1. Processing chain

All products are generated by
[`mapbiomas_brazil_deforestation_secondary_vegetation.py`](../mapbiomas_brazil_deforestation_secondary_vegetation.py),
exported per 1:1,000,000 CIM block (49 blocks, 300 m buffer), at 30 m, with `MODE` pyramiding.
Each step writes an `ImageCollection` under
`projects/mapbiomas-brazil/assets/LAND-COVER/COLLECTION-11/DEFORESTATION/` and is filtered by the
`version` property in the next step.

```
C11 integration (classification-ft, 0-4-14-c84-5)
  └─ RUN_DEF              deforestation-secondary-vegetation          temporal rules
      └─ RUN_DEF_FT       deforestation-secondary-vegetation-ft       spatial filter (11 px, year by year)
          ├─ RUN_DEF_ANNUAL       …-annual                            transition*100 + coverage(year-1)
          │   └─ RUN_DEF_ACCUMULATED  …-accumulated                   first event per period
          ├─ RUN_DEF_FREQUENCY    deforestation-frequency             number of years with deforestation
          └─ RUN_SV_AGE           secondary-vegetation-age            consecutive years in secondary veg.
```

| Constant | Value in C11 |
|---|---|
| `VERSION_INPUT_INTEGRATION` | `0-4-14-c84-5` |
| `VERSION_OUTPUT_*` (all products) | `0-4-14-c84-5-2` |
| `YEARS` | 1985–2025 (41 years) |
| `GROUP_SIZE_FILTER` / `GROUP_SIZE_EXCEPTION_FILTER` | 11 / 11 pixels |
| `PERIODS_ALL_YEARS` | every `[start, end]` pair with start in 1987–2025 — 780 periods |

The export **skips** assets that already exist. A new round requires bumping `VERSION_OUTPUT_*`.

## 2. Transition legend

| Code | Class |
|---|---|
| 0 | No data |
| 1 | Anthropic |
| 2 | Primary vegetation |
| 3 | Secondary vegetation |
| 4 | Primary vegetation suppression |
| 5 | Recovery to secondary vegetation |
| 6 | Secondary vegetation suppression |
| 7 | Other transitions |

How the land cover classes aggregate into these codes is in `CLASS_LOOKUP`, at the top of the
script, and in [temporal-rules.md §1.1](temporal-rules.md#11-aggregation-of-the-original-classes).

## 3. Why the public series starts in 1987

The rules that write classes 3 and 5 have `min_start=1`: they need one year of history before the
window. 1985 and 1986 are therefore **warm-up** years — the pipeline processes them, but the public
data starts in 1987. See [temporal-rules.md §4](temporal-rules.md#4-the-min_start-parameter).

## 4. Public assets

Folder: `projects/mapbiomas-public/assets/brazil/lulc/collection11/`, publicly readable.
Checked in Earth Engine on 2026-10-15.

| Asset | Bands | Type | Content |
|---|---|---|---|
| `mapbiomas_brazil_collection11_deforestation_secondary_vegetation_v5` | 39 — `classification_1987` … `classification_2025` | `uint8` | Filtered transitions (codes 0–7). **Official C11 product** |
| `mapbiomas_brazil_collection11_deforestation_secondary_vegetation_by_coverage_v1` | 39 — `classification_1987` … `classification_2025` | `uint16` | `transition*100 + coverage(year-1)` |

- `v5` is the `…-ft` product (round `0-4-14-c84-5-2`) clipped to 1987–2025 and mosaicked. `v4`
  only went up to 2024 and was unpublished on 2026-08-18.
- In `by_coverage`, the **two land cover digits** use the public C11 legend (`coverage_v3`), which
  publishes class 13 as **77** — the asset records `class_remap="13->77"` and
  `pixel_encoding="transition*100 + coverage(year-1)"`. Example: `477` is primary vegetation
  suppression (4) on a pixel that was class 77 the year before.
- **Not published:** the C11 accumulated, frequency and secondary vegetation age products exist
  only as internal assets (round `0-4-14-c84-5-2`).

## 5. Encoding of each product

### 5.1 Transitions (`…-secondary-vegetation`, `…-ft`, public `v5`)

One `classification_{year}` band per year, codes 0–7 from section 2. `-ft` differs from the raw
product only by the spatial filter: deforestation (4, 6) or secondary vegetation (3, 5) patches
with up to 11 connected pixels **in the same year** become 7.

### 5.2 Annual crossed with land cover (`…-annual`, public `by_coverage_v1`)

`classification_{year} = transition(year) * 100 + coverage(year - 1)`. The land cover is that of
the **previous** year, which tells *what* was suppressed or where the secondary vegetation started
from. The internal product starts in 1986 (the first year with a previous land cover) and uses the
internal land cover codes (13); the public one starts in 1987, with the `13 → 77` remap.

### 5.3 Accumulated (`…-accumulated`, internal)

For each of the 780 periods, two bands:

- `deforestation_accumulated_{start}_{end}` — the annual value (5.2) of the **first** deforestation
  in the period; primary vegetation suppression (4xx) takes precedence over secondary (6xx);
- `secondary_vegetation_accumulated_{start}_{end}` — the annual value of the first secondary
  vegetation occurrence (3xx) in the period; failing that, the recovery (5xx) of the last year.

1,560 bands, masked where there is no event.

### 5.4 Frequency (`deforestation-frequency`, internal)

`deforestation_frequency_{start}_{end}`: how many years in the period the pixel had class 4 or 6.
780 bands, `uint8`, masked where the frequency is zero.

### 5.5 Secondary vegetation age (`secondary-vegetation-age`, internal)

`secondary_vegetation_age_{year}` — consecutive years in class 3 or 5 up to that year, reset to
zero when the pixel leaves those classes: `age(t) = (age(t-1) + sv(t)) * sv(t)`. 41 bands,
`uint8`. Note the band prefix: it is **not** `classification_`.
